Types of Bullying

Bullying takes many forms — all of them hurt

Physical

Hitting, kicking, damaging property

Verbal

Teasing, name-calling, insults

Social

Exclusion, rumors, public embarrassment

Cyberbullying

Online harassment, mean messages

Being Bullied?

  • • Unexplainable injuries
  • • Frequent headaches or stomach aches
  • • Avoiding school or social situations
  • • Declining grades or loss of interest

Bullying Others?

  • • Getting into fights frequently
  • • Having friends who bully others
  • • Becoming increasingly aggressive
  • • Frequent detentions or suspensions
